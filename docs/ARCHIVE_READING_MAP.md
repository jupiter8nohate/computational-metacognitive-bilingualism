## Five editorial invariants

1. **PROFILE != PERSON:** The human being retains the authority to describe their meaning.
2. **PATTERN != PROOF:** Similarities are hypotheses until appropriately tested.
3. **SOURCE != INTERPRETATION:** A linked source must support the precise claim being made.
4. **FICTION != HISTORICAL REPORT:** Narrative invention remains explicitly labeled.
5. **HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY:** The right to question a decision remains central.
