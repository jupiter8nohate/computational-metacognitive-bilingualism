# D.N.A. Bible™ // Editorial and Recovery Protocol

**Purpose:** Keep literary ambition, attribution, source discipline, accessibility, and Recovery coupled to tests rather than promotional claims.

## Canon boundary

- The five existing 2084 transmissions remain the published canonical arc.
- New codex portal scenes are supplemental and are marked as such.
- Companion scrolls and the Sacred Error repository book keep their distinct status.
- Machine-generated Living Codex reflections never automatically become canonical material.
- Author acceptance is an explicit human editorial decision, not a score threshold.
- Six volumes of 111 pages form a proposed 666-page architecture, not a finished manuscript.

## Continuity and revision

For any future chapter: identify point of view, sensory opening, dramatic need, obstacle, reversals, human consequence, and one meaningful unresolved question. Prefer scene and dialogue over repetitive manifesto exposition. Maintain Nia's turning blue bird, the seventh chair, 03:14, and Mara's evidence discipline. A symbol may have multiple literary interpretations without becoming historical proof.

Canonical editions use source-controlled changes with review. Distinguish additions, corrections, and reinterpretations. Release artifact signing and SHA-256 integrity only cover the exact published bytes of a matching release; signatures do not demonstrate factual truth or confer ownership by themselves.

## Dual reading pathways

STORY is continuous fiction. WITNESS displays interpretive notes and source categories. The portal keeps both pathways readable without JavaScript; enhancement is optional.

The interactive Seven Gates are fictional local browser choices. Do not store, transmit, rank, or profile visitors based on their decisions. Accessibility is never conditioned on consent to analytics. External sources are links for examination, not proof of the story's events.

## CI acceptance criteria

1. Canon and evidence JSON parse and satisfy the canon schema.
2. The five original transmissions appear exactly once, in sequence.
3. No indexed route points outside the repository or to traversal paths.
4. Every internal route corresponds to an authored or published path.
5. Every evidence edge references known nodes and a defined relation.
6. The seven fictional gates have unique letters and a clear human-appeal choice.
7. Browser widgets use text nodes, not unsanitized raw HTML rendering.
8. Navigation and reader controls remain usable without motion, tracking, or mouse gestures.
9. MkDocs strict-build, link integrity, and existing repository tests pass.
10. A protected-main pull request is reviewed before merge.

## Recovery

If the interactive enhancement fails, static Markdown links and narrative remain available. If JSON cannot load, the original five transmission links remain present. If the publishing workflow fails, leave the prior deployed edition intact. On a bad release, document the revision and revert through Git rather than erasing provenance.

```text
PATTERN != PROOF
FICTION != FACT
AUTOMATION != AUTHORSHIP
SIGNATURE != FACTUAL_VALIDATION
HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
```
