## Prologue: Who Is Permitted to Observe Whom?

The history of the eye is not only the history of sight. Across religion, philosophy, architecture, statecraft, engineering, art, and computing, the eye has represented protection, justice, knowledge, vigilance, power, healing, punishment, memory, and control.

CMB treats that history as a genealogy of observation:

~~~text
PROTECTIVE EYE
    ↓
DIVINE WITNESS
    ↓
MORAL WITNESS
    ↓
INNER EYE
    ↓
PANOPTIC EYE
    ↓
ELECTRONIC EYE
    ↓
ALGORITHMIC EYE
    ↓
WORLD RETINA
    ↓
RECIPROCAL EYE
~~~

The final transformation is the CMB contribution:

> The machine may look at the world, but humanity retains the right to look back into the machine.

# BOOK I // THE EYE BEFORE THE MACHINE

## 1. The Apotropaic Eye

Protective-eye traditions such as the Nazar Boncugu and eye-bearing protective amulets are useful CMB precedents because the gaze is defensive rather than extractive.

~~~text
PANOPTIC LOGIC:
HUMAN = TARGET

APOTROPAIC CMB LOGIC:
THREAT_TO_HUMAN = TARGET
~~~

The CMB protective layer watches for malware, fraud, unauthorized access, data exfiltration, model drift, corrupted provenance, and coercive profiling. It does not treat a machine-generated profile as authority over a person's worth, identity, motives, or destiny.

## 2. Wedjat: The Recovery Eye

The Wedjat, commonly called the Eye of Horus, is used here as a CMB symbol of restoration and wholeness.

~~~text
FRACTURE
  -> DETECT
  -> BACKTRACE
  -> RESTORE
  -> VERIFY
  -> DOCUMENT
  -> CONTINUE
~~~

CMB name: **WEDJAT_RECOVERY**.

The mapping is symbolic. It does not redefine Egyptian religion.

## 3. The Solar Eye

The Eye of Ra and solar-justice imagery associated with ancient divine observation provide a symbolic contrast between restoration and threat response.

~~~text
WEDJAT = RECOVER
RA     = RESPOND

RECOVERY_CHANNEL != THREAT_RESPONSE_CHANNEL
~~~

The engineering lesson is separation of duties. A subsystem that repairs information should not automatically control consequential action.

~~~text

        ↓

        ↓
MODEL
        ↓

PIXELS != WORLD

~~~

## 13. Planetary Observation

Satellites, radar, weather instruments, environmental stations, public sensor feeds, and geospatial models create a distributed planetary sensing layer.

The useful CMB target is Earth systems: climate, ecology, infrastructure, wildfire, flooding, glaciers, vegetation, transportation, supply chains, and disaster response rather than permanent individualized behavioral control.

# BOOK VII // THE WEAPONIZED EYE

Military and intelligence analogies reveal a central category error: effectors are often confused with sensors.

~~~text
SENSE
  -> FUSE
  -> REMEMBER
  -> DETECT
  -> HYPOTHESIZE
  -> VERIFY
  -> AUTHORIZED_DECISION
  -> ACT
~~~

Jammers, interceptors, kinetic systems, and other force-bearing systems are effectors.

~~~text
SENSOR != WEAPON
OBSERVATION != TARGETING
ANOMALY != HOSTILITY
MODEL_CONFIDENCE != AUTHORIZATION
~~~

Named military systems belong in the research claim matrix with evidence states such as documented, reported, developmental, disputed, manufacturer claim, speculative analogy, and unverified.

This book does not provide targeting or weapons-operation instructions.
