## Overview

**Jupiter Hudson**, who also publishes as **WisdomLoveThePoet** and **Jupiter 8**, is an American writer and independent creative technologist whose work brings together programming-language notation, philosophy, speculative literature, and disability-rights advocacy. His published project, **Computational Metacognitive Bilingualism™ (CMB™)**, explores how people can communicate with computational systems without surrendering human interpretation, consent or self-definition.

Rather than limiting code to executable software, Hudson also employs it as a literary medium. Arrows, assertions, conditional logic, symbolic glyphs and structured documents become vehicles for examining the authority of predictive systems. A recurring principle of his work is `HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY`. The notation expresses an ethical position, **not** a mathematical proof or an existing rule of law.

CMB's proposed contribution lies in combining human-authored context, machine-readable policy, provenance, system controls, and reviewable records, while acknowledging older traditions of code poetry, accessibility research, human-computer interaction and digital-rights advocacy. For the scope and limits of those claims, see [CMB distinction](CMB_DISTINCTION.md), [prior art and positioning](PRIOR_ART_AND_POSITIONING.md), and the [research dissertation](dissertation/CMB_COGNITIVE_SOVEREIGNTY_DISSERTATION.md).

## Life experience and advocacy

Hudson has written publicly about living with autism and mental-health experiences. In his [first-person testimony](lived-experience.md), he discusses how his own experiences inform his attention to patterns, explicit rules, uncertainty and verification. He does not present his personal cognitive experiences as universal traits of autistic people or people with other diagnoses.

His work argues that a disability label or algorithmic classification should not be allowed to stand in for a full account of a person. It advocates accessible public services, effective communication, meaningful opportunities to challenge decisions, and safeguards against inappropriate profiling.

### Reported encounter with Chapel Hill law enforcement

Hudson alleges that he suffered fractured ribs during an encounter involving law enforcement in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, and says his disability and mental-health circumstances were not adequately accounted for. He has stated that he sought responses from North Carolina political leadership and has used the experience to advocate for crisis-response training, independent review and accessible grievance procedures.

**Evidence boundary:** This paragraph reports **Hudson's account**. The project archive does not, by this biography alone, establish the date and circumstances of the encounter, the identity of responding personnel, the medical cause of the injuries, the handling of any complaints, or any judicial finding of misconduct. Such determinations would require case-specific records and independent examination. [Read the author's lived-experience page](lived-experience.md).

The broader legal-policy question concerns how disability accommodations and constitutional limits on police force apply to particular facts. The two legal inquiries are related but distinct. Hudson's [cognitive-sovereignty research](dissertation/CMB_COGNITIVE_SOVEREIGNTY_DISSERTATION.md) is presented as developing scholarship, not a court ruling.

## Computational Metacognitive Bilingualism™

CMB describes a translation process between human meaning and machine-readable representations. Its recurring propositions include:

| Principle | Interpretation within CMB |
| --- | --- |
| `PATTERN != PROOF` | A detected correlation requires investigation before it supports a factual conclusion. |
| `PROFILE != PERSON` | Records and classifications provide incomplete representations of individuals. |
| `MODEL != MIND` | A computational model is not the entirety of the mind or person it models. |
| `PREDICTION != DESTINY` | A forecast is not an inevitable personal future. |
| `ACCESS != CONSENT` | Using a service does not by itself justify unrelated collection or use of personal data. |

The principles serve as philosophical and design constraints. Their application in software requires actual implementation; their application in court requires an independent legal basis. [Read the CMB concept introduction](concepts/what-is-cmb.md).

## Creative and literary work

**The Flamingoglyph Code (FGC)** is Hudson's authored symbolic-language project for exploring expression, rights, provenance and human-machine interpretation. [FGC overview](concepts/flamingoglyph-code.md).

**Err ⃝or⃟⃤ GLITCHOLOGY** is a code-art and experimental-language project that uses the appearance of errors and unusual glyphs to explore ambiguity, incomplete categories and the limits of machine classification. [Origin and creator statement](ERR_GLITCHOLOGY_ORIGIN.md).

**The D.N.A. Bible™** is a speculative literary archive. The title's internal expansion, **Demons Need Attention**, frames attention extraction and compulsory engagement as themes for fiction and social criticism. [D.N.A. reader map](ARCHIVE_READING_MAP.md).

**DEATH NOTE 2084** is a connected dystopian narrative about classification, automation, compulsory consent and human agency. Its invented systems, including L.U.C.I.F.E.R.™, are fictional and analytical devices, **not proof of an actual centralized surveillance network**. [Start the 2084 story](2084/index.md).

## Cognitive sovereignty and public policy

Hudson uses **cognitive sovereignty** to describe the principle that computational tools may aid people without gaining unrestricted authority over identity, meaning and judgment. His work examines **coercive bundling**: the risk that an essential service may be packaged with data practices or digital interfaces that a user cannot reasonably avoid.

A proposed **Functional Refusal Architecture** would retain access to necessary services when optional profiling is declined, where technically and legally appropriate, with accessible alternatives and human review. It is a policy and research proposal; it is **not** an automatically enforceable right under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act. Whether a particular public system violates the ADA or another law depends on the governing legal standards and evidence.

See [Cognitive Sovereignty](concepts/cognitive-sovereignty.md) and [Research Position](CMB_RESEARCH_POSITION.md).
