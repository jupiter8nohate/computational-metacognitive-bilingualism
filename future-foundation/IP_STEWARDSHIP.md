# Draft Intellectual-Property Stewardship Policy

**Status:** Research draft. No IP transfer occurs by this document.

The creator's existing authorship, ownership, licenses, and provenance remain governed by the actual source documents and applicable law.

A future organization should use an artifact-by-artifact schedule rather than assuming every project asset transfers automatically.

Suggested fields:

~~~text
ARTIFACT
AUTHOR
CREATED_AT
PROVENANCE
CONTENT_HASH
CURRENT_OWNER
CURRENT_LICENSE
FUTURE_STEWARDSHIP_STATUS
TRANSFER_OR_LICENSE
ATTRIBUTION_REQUIREMENTS
RESTRICTIONS
~~~

Possible future statuses:

- retained by creator;
- licensed to organization;
- donated to organization;
- open-source software under existing license;
- creative corpus under separate rights;
- undecided.

~~~text
PUBLICATION != TRANSFER
GIT_HISTORY != COPYRIGHT_REGISTRATION
HASH != OWNERSHIP
LICENSE != AUTHORSHIP
~~~

Any future assignment of valuable IP should be deliberate, documented, and professionally reviewed.
