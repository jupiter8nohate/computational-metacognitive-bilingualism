## The signs worth remembering

The time **03:14**. The **crooked blue bird**. The **pink feather**. The **seventh chair**. A machine finally answering **UNKNOWN**.

These are narrative motifs, not cryptographic proof, diagnoses, or predictions. Their connections are maintained in the [story continuity guide](continuity.md).
