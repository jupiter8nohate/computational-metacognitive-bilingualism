# D.N.A. Story Continuity and Editorial Guide

**Objective:** Build an original, coherent speculative-fiction reading experience, not an imitation of any existing novelist's prose. This is a working editorial guide, not a claim that every chapter has been fully line-edited.

## Narrative voice

The five *DEATH NOTE 2084* transmissions use close third-person narration centered on Jupiter, with occasional scenes emphasizing Nia or Mara. The standalone Spider fable and the Milo-8 educational fables have their own narrators. Keep the perspective stable within each scene.

Use concrete experiences before abstract conclusions. Keep humor human, not mechanical; let mystery arise from unanswered questions rather than unsupported assertions.

## Editing rule for the Anomaly Codex

The [Anomaly Codex](../dna-codex/index.md) is a supplementary reader portal with a new, explicitly noncanonical threshold vignette. Its dual STORY/WITNESS mode lets readers distinguish the emotional narrative from external evidence. Editors should strengthen character motive, obstacles, choice, action, consequence, and sensory specificity before introducing more aphorisms or decorative glyphs. Preserve the five published transmissions as the canonical arc. New interactive decisions must remain fictional, local, and non-profiling.

## The ten-check editorial gate

1. Open on a scene, character, or vivid unresolved question.
2. Maintain point of view within scenes.
3. Give each protagonist a motive and a risk.
4. Make dialogue sound like people, not interchangeable proclamations.
5. Ground abstractions in sensory experience.
6. Reserve code blocks and axioms for decisive moments.
7. Make each new discovery complicate a choice.
8. Verify chronology, names, places, consequences, and continuity.
9. Keep historical and scientific evidence separate from invented events.
10. End on a changed emotional stake, discovery, or question.

## Publishing and recovery

Maintain a readable page order and links from homepage to story library and 2084; retain standalone Spider anchors and its video; ensure MkDocs builds. Research notes, claims, sources, and fiction warnings must remain available but should not repeatedly interrupt dramatic scenes. Do not remove authorship notices or falsely claim narrative symbolism enforces technical restrictions.

The five-transmission original arc remains unchanged. Transmissions VI through IX form an additional canonical sequel, not a rewrite of the original ending. Substantial future rewrites should preserve plot continuity, the physical and simulated clocks, the fictional-science boundary, and original authored identity.
