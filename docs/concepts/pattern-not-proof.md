# Pattern != Proof

**Canonical definition.** PATTERN != PROOF is CMB's epistemic boundary that a detected correlation, recurring signal, similarity, or statistical pattern may be evidence but is not automatically sufficient proof of a stronger claim.

## Formal expression

```text
PATTERN != PROOF
```

## Why it matters

Automated systems are optimized to detect regularities. CMB requires the evidentiary strength of a conclusion to match the evidence available instead of silently converting pattern recognition into certainty.

## Example

A recommender may detect that someone frequently reads a topic. That pattern does not by itself prove the person's identity, intent, beliefs, diagnosis, or future behavior.

## Interpretation boundaries

The principle does not say patterns are useless. Patterns can motivate hypotheses and bounded decisions. Strong claims still require context, appropriate evidence, uncertainty handling, and human judgment.
