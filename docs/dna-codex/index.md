<article class="dna-scene">
<p>At 03:14, the House of a Million Doors began charging admission to a room nobody had built.</p>
<p>Jupiter discovered this while standing behind Nia in the queue for a library card. The queue was nine citizens long and moved backward whenever somebody agreed to the privacy policy.</p>
<p>“Good news,” he said. “They finally found a way to make reading illegal without banning books.”</p>
<p>Mara looked at the sign above the counter. It said <em>FREE KNOWLEDGE. PERMANENT BEHAVIORAL RECORD REQUIRED.</em></p>
<p>“Not illegal,” she replied. “Just conditional.”</p>
<p>Nia unfolded her blue bird. One wing curved away from the diagram supplied by the Ministry.</p>
<p>The terminal scanned it and displayed: <strong>ERROR: IMPROPER FLIGHT MODEL.</strong></p>
<p>“The bird is turning,” Nia said.</p>
<p><em>“Your interpretation is not an available option.”</em></p>
<p>Nia tapped the blank space beside ACCEPT. “Then make room.”</p>
</article>
