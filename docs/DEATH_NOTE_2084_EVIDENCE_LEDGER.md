## 01 // AN EVIDENCE STANDARD THAT CAN BE AUDITED

| Marker | What it means | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DOCUMENTED** | A named source records an actual deployed practice, product capability, legal rule, study, or regulator action. | Every provider, country, or institution does it. |
| **CONTEXT-DEPENDENT** | A technology is real, but its use, scope, rights consequences, or deployment vary. | A universal coercive system has been demonstrated. |
| **RESEARCH / PROTOTYPE** | Publications or experiments establish a method or investigational capability. | General consumer deployment or proven harmful use. |
| **FICTIONAL EXTENSION** | The D.N.A. story combines, magnifies, or extrapolates technologies for narrative purposes. | Independent historical evidence. |

The source status of a specific statement is narrower than the status of the whole topic. **A verified component cannot verify an imagined completed system.**

**Interpretation rule:** A product description verifies what a provider says its tool can do, not independently measured prevalence or outcomes. A regulator's complaint is an allegation unless an adjudication or settlement establishes a more specific result; a settlement is not necessarily an admission of liability. An academic prototype is not deployment evidence.

## 03 // THE THREE TRAPS THAT CONNECT THE TEN

### A. THE "I AGREE" TRAP // BUNDLED COERCION

A beneficial service can be paired with optional data collection under a design that makes refusing the tracking practically costly. This can create pressure to accept unwanted terms. The FTC's findings about limited opt-outs and data-intensive business incentives give that concern a documented basis. They do not establish that every service requires unlawful consent.

~~~text
HELPFUL_SERVICE + UNNECESSARY_TRACKING
    |
    v
TAKE_BOTH_OR_LOSE_ACCESS
    |
    v
CONSENT_QUALITY_QUESTIONED

DESIGN REQUIREMENT:
SEPARATE_NECESSARY_PROCESSING_FROM_OPTIONAL_PROFILING
~~~

### B. THE PREDICTIVE DEATH LOOP // SCORE TO VERDICT

A model estimates risk. An institution turns the estimate into a consequential decision. In a badly governed setting, the decision can create data that later models treat as independent confirmation. That is a **potential feedback-loop failure mode**, not a demonstrated universal control system.

~~~text
DATA -> MODEL -> SCORE -> DECISION
                     |        |
                     |        v
                     +---- NEW RECORD
                              |
                              v
                          NEXT MODEL

COUNTERMEASURE:
ACCURACY + REASONS + CONTESTABILITY + INDEPENDENT REVIEW
~~~

### C. DIFFERENCE != DEFECT // THE CROOKED BIRD

If a benchmark expects one normative behavior, a student or applicant may be mislabeled when their behavior differs for an irrelevant reason. The DOJ's disability-hiring guidance and research into remote-proctoring false flags show why accessibility and individual context cannot simply be treated as noise.

**A model's failure to explain a person is a model limitation, not proof of a person's defect.**

## 07 // THE CROOKED BIRD IS THE STANDARD

Nia's bird was never crooked because a machine had failed to count its feathers.

It was crooked because the machine assumed every bird was flying in a straight line.

The institution was free to measure angles. It was not entitled to infer the meaning of the flight without hearing the artist.

This is the D.N.A. Bible's governing distinction:

> The machine may produce a probability. A person retains the right to challenge what the probability is being used to decide.

The stronger warning is not **"fiction has become fact."**

It is **"documented technologies make these governance questions urgent even though the story is fictional."**
