# 1. Biological Scaffold

The biological layer is treated as a measured living system, not as a semantic shortcut for a complete human brain.

## CMB boundary

```text
NEURAL TISSUE
    !=
WHOLE BRAIN
    !=
MIND
    !=
PERSON
```

The term **organoid** refers here to an in-vitro three-dimensional neural-tissue model used in research. It may reproduce selected cellular, structural, or network properties relevant to neuroscience, but it is not equivalent to a full human nervous system.

## Verification variable

`B` represents a declared biological-state measurement chosen by a study, for example a normalized viability, stability, or network-state metric.

CMB does not prescribe a biological assay. The verifier receives an already defined numerical observation and checks it against an already declared reference and tolerance.

```text
B_observed - B_reference = R_bio
```

The meaning of `B` must be documented by the researcher. A low residual cannot prove that the biological model is complete, conscious, cognitively equivalent to a human, or suitable for conclusions outside the stated experiment.

## Interpretation invariant

```text
BIOLOGICAL_SIMILARITY != IDENTITY
MODEL_FIDELITY != PERSONHOOD
MEASURED_STATE != TOTAL_STATE
```
