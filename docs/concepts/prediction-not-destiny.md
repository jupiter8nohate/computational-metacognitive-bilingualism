# Prediction != Destiny

**Canonical definition.** PREDICTION != DESTINY means a probabilistic forecast about a person's likely future is not the same thing as an inevitable outcome or a legitimate authority to determine that future.

## Formal expression

```text
PREDICTION != DESTINY
```

## Why it matters

Predictions can influence decisions, and those decisions can become self-reinforcing. CMB preserves room for change, appeal, uncertainty, context, and human agency.

## Example

A model may estimate a probability that a user will take an action. That forecast should not be treated as proof that the action will occur or as justification to erase the user's opportunity to choose otherwise.

## Interpretation boundaries

The principle does not reject forecasting. It rejects the conversion of probabilistic output into inevitability, identity, or unreviewable authority.
