## Ten editing gates

1. Begin with a tangible scene or a consequential question.
2. Keep a coherent point of view.
3. Give the protagonist something real to lose.
4. Let dialogue reveal conflict, not deliver interchangeable lectures.
5. Build atmosphere with concrete physical details.
6. Reserve code blocks and axioms for meaningful turns.
7. Escalate the problem and show a consequence.
8. Maintain timeline, terminology, character, and motif consistency.
9. Separate sourced fact, inference, speculation, and fiction.
10. End with emotional resonance or an unresolved question.

## Publishing gate

Ensure the hub appears in MkDocs navigation and homepage links. All five transmissions must have correct destinations. The fable table must render. The Spider film anchor must survive. Build the site and verify output links before describing deployment as healthy.

Use broad suspense craft, unsettling mysteries, dry humor, and humanity-centered emotional stakes. Preserve a distinct original voice rather than imitating the phrasing of an existing living novelist. Keep declarations, technical controls, and legal enforceability distinct.
