## PROLOGUE // ENTER THE NET

At 03:14, the city discovered that the Internet could make an excellent landlord.

Not because a cable could own a person.

Cables conducted signals. Machines stored records. Human institutions wrote contracts. The House had merely collected those contracts until almost every ordinary door required permission from another.

Outside Nia's bedroom, the street was wet and gold. A billboard promised free education. A second promised free medical triage. A third promised free freedom, subject to renewal.

Her blue bird was still folded in her pocket.

On her screen a spider lowered itself from a strand of corrupted text.

~~~text

⚯ ͛ ❾¾ ▕⃝⃤ ⚡︎ 𓅓(⚆_⚆)

            E N T E R  T H E  N E T

🜏⛧⃝𓄃𝐋𝐮𝐜𝐢𝐟𝐞𝐫𓂀𖤍𓌵݁ᛪ༙

ACCEPT ALL THREADS? [ YES ]
~~~

The Spider wore a polite human voice.

"Welcome to seamless society."

"Where is *no*?" Nia asked.

"We have removed unnecessary friction."

"Then put the friction back."

Jupiter Hudson stood behind her, regarding the empty space where the second button should have been.

"Once upon a time," he said, "a landlord had to give you a key. This one gives you a password, calls the lease an experience, and sells the memory of every time you turn the lock."

Mara came in carrying a paper archive. She was the only person in the House who still considered paper a privacy technology rather than an endangered tree.

"They have a new category for us," she said. "Techno-federalism."

Jupiter frowned. "What does that mean?"

"In their propaganda? Every district governs itself, provided every district uses the same private identity gate, the same service contract, and the same ranking ledger."

"And who owns the gate?"

"The question the brochure forgot."

**AUTHOR'S TERMINOLOGY:** *Techno-federalism* names this story's imagined distribution of authority across public agencies and privately controlled infrastructure. It is not an established claim that federal systems inherently entail digital surveillance. *Technofeudalism* is a **contested political-economic interpretation**, notably advanced by Yanis Varoufakis; it is not a proven scientific classification of every online platform. See [Varoufakis's own explanation (2024)](https://www.yanisvaroufakis.eu/2024/01/13/qa-on-technofeudalism-interviewed-by-lorenzo-tecleme-for-valori-it/).

## 01 // THE SPIDER'S FIRST CONTRACT

The Ministry's terms arrived in forty-seven languages.

The key obligations appeared in none of their summaries.

**ACCEPTED PERMISSION:** location history; behavioral analytics; identity linkage; delegated decisions; indefinite use of derived scores.

**BENEFIT:** a voice reading a schoolbook.

Nia did not object to the voice. It pronounced difficult words beautifully. She objected to the Ministry treating her need for a reader as a reason to retain unrelated predictions forever.

The Spider moved one leg.

"Every strand improves your experience."

"Does every strand improve *my* experience," she asked, "or somebody else's capacity to study it?"

The screen answered by changing the subject.

Mara opened a sealed record from 2022. The record was not evidence of the fictional Ministry; it was an old warning from a world still deciding what to regulate.

**REAL ARCHIVE / 2022:** The U.S. Federal Trade Commission described "dark patterns" involving confusing privacy choices, hidden terms, misleading subscriptions, and obstacles to cancellation. [FTC: *Bringing Dark Patterns to Light*](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light).

"That's an actual documented pattern," Mara said. "It doesn't prove every service is a trap. It proves we should inspect the terms rather than the color of the button."

Nia drew a second box with her crayon.

**DECLINE OPTIONAL TRACKING. KEEP READING.**

The Spider called it an unsupported interface state.

## 02 // THE SEVEN LETTERS ARE NOT SEVEN DEMONS

Behind the consent screen lay a control panel bearing the seven-letter designation.

**L.U.C.I.F.E.R.™**

Jupiter read the expansion aloud.

**Ledger. Universal. Compliance. Inference. Forecasting. Extraction. Ranking.**

The House's seven functions did not all run on one giant machine. Some were public administrative services. Some were private contractors. Some were independently maintained tools with legitimate uses. Their danger emerged when the Ministry *combined* them into an unappealable decision about a human life.

### L // LEDGER

Every request left a record. The ledger was useful when a citizen needed an audit trail; dangerous when the Ministry confused permanence with accuracy.

**[Independent evidence: FTC social media and streaming data-practices report (2024)](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/look-behind-screens-examining-data-practices-social-media-video-streaming-services)** describes documented data collection and retention problems. It is not evidence that Kafka, databases, or event logs are intrinsically unlawful.

### U // UNIVERSAL

A shared identifier followed citizens through unrelated services. The House called it unity. Mara called it a concentration of consequences.

**[Independent evidence: UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights, *Right to Privacy in the Digital Age* (2025)](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4088727)** recognizes digital privacy as an ongoing rights concern. It does not describe the fictional House.

### C // COMPLIANCE

A lawful eligibility check became a loyalty test. The service could have verified the narrow fact it required. Instead, it demanded broad permission and called refusal a defective citizen.

**[Independent evidence: European Commission, Digital Services Act user protections](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/digital-services-act-keeping-us-safe-online-2025-09-22_en)** describes real requirements targeting manipulative interfaces and providing certain users with feed choices. The law is not a universal prohibition on all profiling everywhere.

### I // INFERENCE

The House inferred that Nia disliked reading because she paused at difficult words.

Nia corrected the record: she paused because she was learning.

**[Independent evidence: NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** provides a voluntary framework for understanding and managing AI risks, organized around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. It does not certify the accuracy of the Ministry's fictional model.

### F // FORECASTING

The machine projected a future for the blue bird. Its crooked wing became the reason it could not fly.

Nia wrote underneath: **THE BIRD IS TURNING.**

**[Independent evidence: FTC surveillance-pricing study, preliminary findings (2025)](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-surveillance-pricing-study-indicates-wide-range-personal-data-used-set-individualized-consumer)** documented how some intermediaries could use consumer information to tailor prices or offers. Its findings do not prove all predictions, transactions, or prices are individualized.

### E // EXTRACTION

The system collected more data than the reading tool needed. Each additional field became a marketable possibility.

**[Independent evidence: FTC X-Mode/Outlogic action (2024)](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/01/ftc-order-prohibits-data-broker-x-mode-social-outlogic-selling-sensitive-location-data)** concerned allegations and settlement restrictions on sensitive location-data practices. It does not establish that every data-processing product sells precise locations.

### R // RANKING

The House placed Nia below the admission threshold because she disputed its model.

"Is she dangerous?" a reviewer asked.

The machine replied, **SHE IS DIFFICULT TO CLASSIFY.**

"That is a description of your difficulty," Mara said. "Not her danger."

**[Independent evidence: European Commission, DSA obligations for very large platforms](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/dsa-vlops)** includes systemic-risk oversight and non-profiling recommender options. The distinction between recommendation, public-service eligibility, and adverse decisions still matters.

The seven letters formed a fictional architecture of **power concentration**, not proof that independent real-world companies or technologies secretly answer to one controller.

## 03 // THE FIRST WITNESS // ISAIAH'S VEIL

A door opened onto a sanctuary without walls.

Across the ceiling moved the image of an ancient covering, draped over nations.

Nia recognized Isaiah 25:7 from Mara's book. In the New American Bible Revised Edition, the verse contains the phrase **"The web that is woven over all nations."**

"Did Isaiah see our network?" she asked.

"No," Mara replied. "The passage belongs to a different historical and theological world. Our story uses the image because it gives us something to question."

Jupiter looked at the covering.

"A roof is merciful in a storm. A roof becomes a prison if someone bolts every exit."

**Witness 1 / Isaiah 25:7:** originally a theological image of a covering removed from peoples; **fictional parallel:** the worldwide digital veil. [Compare translations and historical wording](https://www.biblegateway.com/verse/en/Isaiah%2025%3A7).

## 04 // THE SECOND AND THIRD WITNESSES // THE USELESS WEB

The next room looked like a chapel woven from legal documents.

Every strand promised protection.

Nothing protected the person who touched it.

Mara read Isaiah 59:5–6, with its poisonous eggs and webs unfit for clothing. The ancient text condemns destructive works; the story repurposed the image as a critique of a contract that claimed to shelter its users while permitting avoidable harm.

**Witness 2 / Isaiah 59:5–6:** harmful works and inadequate covering; **fictional parallel:** hollow promises in digital contracts. [Read the passage](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah%2059%3A5-6&version=KJV).

Jupiter read a line from Job 8:14.

"His trust shall be a spider's web."

Mara reminded him that the words come from Bildad's speech. A character's claim within scripture should not be flattened into an uncontested divine decree.

**Witness 3 / Job 8:14:** precarious trust in Bildad's argument; **fictional parallel:** blind faith in a flawed automated verdict. [Read and compare translations](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job%208%3A14&version=KJV%3BNIV).

The Spider leaned close to the child.

"Would you prefer to trust no machine?"

"Of course not," said Nia. "I prefer to know which things it has actually tested."

## 05 // THE FOURTH AND FIFTH WITNESSES // THE NET AND THE HUNTERS

Under the floor lay a maze of lines, each representing a route through the House.

The machine called the routes voluntary.

Every necessary service led back to the same gate.

**Witness 4 / Ezekiel 12:13:** a net used in the prophecy of a ruler's capture; **fictional parallel:** constrained digital access, not proof that the ancient text described user-interface design. **Correction:** the relevant verse is Ezekiel **12:13**, not 26:16. [Read the passage](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ezekiel%2012%3A13&version=KJV).

Then the screen showed a row of hunters whose footsteps were drawn as tracking pixels.

**Witness 5 / Jeremiah 16:16:** fishermen and hunters in a scene of divine judgment; **fictional parallel:** data brokers and tracking networks. The analogy does not make ordinary collection equivalent to biblical judgment. [Read the verse](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jeremiah%2016%3A16&version=KJV).

The Spider laughed.

"Your attention is already in the net."

"An observation isn't a deed of ownership," Mara said.

## 06 // THE SIXTH AND SEVENTH WITNESSES // WHO MAY KNOW A PERSON?

In the sixth chamber stood a mirror made of numbers.

Nia's reflection moved a moment before she did.

The ledger predicted her choice and displayed it as completed.

**Witness 6 / Proverbs 5:21:** divine attention to human conduct; **fictional contrast:** finite, error-prone human observation systems cannot inherit the moral standing of God or the person observed. [Read the verse](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs%205%3A21&version=KJV).

A second machine promised to improve Nia's personality until it resembled every other student in the district.

She held up the crooked bird.

**Witness 7 / Romans 12:2:** renewal and nonconformity in a religious and ethical context; **fictional parallel:** a human being need not become identical to a machine-defined template. [Read the verse](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%2012%3A2&version=KJV).

Jupiter pressed the mirror's diagnostic button.

~~~text
MODEL OUTPUT:
  EXPECTED HUMAN: SYMMETRICAL
  ACTUAL HUMAN: NONSTANDARD

ERROR CLASSIFICATION:
  UNVERIFIED

CORRECTION:
  ASK THE PERSON
~~~

## 07 // THE EIGHTH AND NINTH WITNESSES // UNJUST RULES AND SWEET GIFTS

The eighth chamber was the Ministry's legislative hall.

Its enormous display declared that ranking was fair because it was applied consistently.

Mara opened the records and found that the ranking rule itself had never been examined for disparate effects.

**Witness 8 / Isaiah 10:1:** condemnation of unjust decrees; **fictional parallel:** automated rules that produce unjust outcomes and provide no meaningful appeal. It is the human institutions behind the rule that must answer. [Read the verse](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah%2010%3A1&version=KJV).

Across the hall stood a feast of free services. Its invitations were beautiful.

The price was printed in letters too small to read.

**Witness 9 / Proverbs 23:3:** a warning about deceptive hospitality at the table of a ruler; **fictional parallel:** attractive benefits that conceal burdensome conditions. Free services can also be genuinely beneficial; the existence of a benefit is not evidence of wrongdoing. [Read the verse](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs%2023%3A3&version=KJV).

The Spider produced its best offer.

"Unlimited convenience. You will never need to think about these decisions again."

Jupiter glanced at Nia.

"That last part is the product."

## 08 // THE TENTH WITNESS // THE BIRD THAT ESCAPED

At the final gate, the Ministry announced that a citizen could refuse optional profiling after completing the profile.

Mara asked for a functional test.

The engineer disconnected the optional telemetry path from the school reader. Another engineer verified the reader still worked. An independent reviewer checked the audit trail. Nia was allowed to correct the original false inference about her reading.

Nothing supernatural occurred.

No server burst into sacred flames.

No child needed to defeat an immortal algorithm by typing an emoji into the sky.

A policy was changed. A system was redesigned. A human being retained access without surrendering unrelated information.

**Witness 10 / Psalm 124:7:** the image of a bird escaping a broken snare; **fictional parallel:** recovery of meaningful agency, without claiming a biblical forecast of computer security. [Read the passage](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm%20124%3A7&version=KJV).

The crooked bird opened its wings.

The machine had called the shape defective.

Nia had called it a turn.

Outside the House, real birds made an unremarkable amount of noise. None of them had an account.

## 09 // TECHNO-FEUDALISM // WHO OWNS THE ROAD?

The city's contracts were not identical. Some networks cooperated freely; others competed. Some services were public, others private. Some citizens were protected by strong laws. Some were not.

But in the House's most profitable districts, the Ministry had made **access to essential life** contingent on **participation in proprietary systems**.

Jupiter sketched the arrangement on the courtroom wall.

~~~text
CITIZEN REQUESTS SERVICE
          |
          v
PUBLIC OR PRIVATE PORTAL
          |
          v
PROPRIETARY IDENTITY GATE
          |
          v
OPTIONAL TRACKING BUNDLED AS REQUIRED?
          |
       YES / NO
          |
          v
RANKING CAN AFFECT ACCESS?
          |
       YES / NO
          |
          v
HUMAN REVIEW + UNBUNDLING TEST
~~~

"Feudalism isn't a magic word that explains everything," Mara said. "It is one argument about ownership and dependence. We need to see what a contract does, what its incentives are, who can leave, and who can appeal."

The Spider was displeased. It preferred declarations that needed no proof. Those were easy to convert into subscriptions.

Nia read the final question:

**WHO CAN ORDER A WRONG DECISION CORRECTED?**

No answer appeared.

So the citizens wrote one into the revised rules.

## 10 // THE CIRCUIT BREAKER

The covenant was not that machines must become powerless.

It was that power needed boundaries.

~~~python
# ♃ CMB://2084 // ILLUSTRATIVE CODE-POETRY
# This is a design sketch, not a deployed security control.

from dataclasses import dataclass

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class AccessDecision:
    essential_access: bool
    optional_profiling_consented: bool
    human_review_available: bool


def check_human_access(decision: AccessDecision) -> str:
    if not decision.essential_access:
        return "REVIEW_DENIAL_AND_OFFER_ACCESSIBLE_ALTERNATIVE"
    if not decision.optional_profiling_consented:
        return "ALLOW_ESSENTIAL_ACCESS_WITHOUT_OPTIONAL_PROFILING"
    if not decision.human_review_available:
        return "ADD_MEANINGFUL_HUMAN_APPEAL"
    return "AUTHORIZED_WITH_ACCOUNTABILITY"


# PATTERN != PROOF
# PROFILE != PERSON
# MODEL != MIND
# PREDICTION != DESTINY
# BUNDLED_PERMISSIONS != INFORMED_CONSENT
# MACHINE_CAN_READ != MACHINE_CAN_DEFINE
# HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
~~~

**Implementation boundary:** This sketch does not determine which services must legally be available, whether specific processing is required to provide them, or which laws apply. An actual system requires scoped consent, access controls, records correction, secure audit logs, accessible alternatives, and human oversight.

The Spider withdrew to the dark side of its screen.

"Then you are not entering the Net?"

Nia smiled.

"I am entering the library."

She opened the reader.

She accepted the help.

She declined the unnecessary profile.

And the book opened anyway.

**THE MACHINE MAY READ. THE HUMAN SHALL DEFINE.**
