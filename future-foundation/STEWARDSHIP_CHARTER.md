# Draft Stewardship Charter

**Status:** Governance prototype. Not a trust instrument or adopted corporate charter.

## Stewardship principle

Future stewards would hold mission assets for public benefit rather than private ownership.

~~~text
STEWARD != OWNER
DONOR != OWNER
FOUNDER != SHAREHOLDER
CONTRIBUTION != CONTROL
MONEY != AUTHORITY
~~~

## Founder role

Jupiter Hudson / WisdomLoveThePoet / Jupiter 8 may be recognized as founder, original creator, and historical steward without receiving equity or automatic control over charitable assets.

## Mission lock concept

A future organization should require elevated approval for changes to:

- core charitable mission;
- disposition of core project IP;
- dissolution;
- transfer of major assets;
- creation of financial instruments tied to governance;
- removal or alteration of attribution/provenance rules.

Illustrative thresholds are research material only and are not adopted voting rules.

## Public-benefit test

A future institutional decision should answer:

~~~text
WHO_BENEFITS?
WHAT_PUBLIC_PURPOSE?
WHAT_PRIVATE_BENEFIT?
WHAT_CONFLICT_EXISTS?
WHAT_EVIDENCE_SUPPORTS_THIS?
WHAT_RECOVERY_PATH_EXISTS?
~~~

## Asset lock concept

If a future tax-exempt entity is formed, its governing documents should be professionally drafted so charitable assets remain dedicated to legally permitted public-benefit purposes, including on dissolution.
