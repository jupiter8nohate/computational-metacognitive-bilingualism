# Community Conduct

CMB welcomes technical criticism, disagreement, research challenges, bug reports,
and alternative interpretations when they allow people to work together.

Participants are expected to discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack
people; avoid harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or targeted abuse;
avoid publishing private information without permission; distinguish critique
from claims about a person's identity, diagnosis, motives, intelligence, or
worth; and report security issues through the security process.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that materially violate
these rules and may restrict participation when necessary.

```text
PROFILE != PERSON
DISAGREEMENT != DEHUMANIZATION
HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
```
