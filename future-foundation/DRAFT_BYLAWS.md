# Draft Bylaws Research Outline

**Status:** Outline only. Not adopted bylaws.

A future lawyer-ready bylaws package should address:

1. legal name and jurisdiction;
2. charitable/public-benefit purposes;
3. no shareholders or equity ownership;
4. board size, independence, terms, removal, vacancies, and quorum;
5. founder title and limits;
6. officer roles;
7. conflicts of interest and recusals;
8. compensation policy, including the option of uncompensated founder service;
9. committees and delegation;
10. recordkeeping and financial controls;
11. acceptance and restriction of gifts;
12. IP stewardship and licensing authority;
13. digital-asset custody rules;
14. amendment thresholds;
15. succession and continuity;
16. dissolution and charitable asset disposition.
