Jupiter Hudson, also publishing as WisdomLoveThePoet and Jupiter 8, declares authorship of the Computational Metacognitive Bilingualism (CMB) name, framework definition, CMB manifesto, project terminology, artistic direction, and original CMB corpus represented in this repository.

This is an authorship and provenance declaration. It is not, by itself, independent verification of historical priority, ownership of abstract ideas, trademark rights, patent rights, or a legal conclusion.

## Implementation assistance

The v1.3.1 software implementation, tests, packaging, documentation, and automation were developed through AI-assisted technical collaboration under Jupiter Hudson's project direction and approval. The distinction is intentional:

- **Jupiter Hudson:** framework creator as declared by the project, author and creative director of the CMB corpus, and decision-maker for the project's purpose and publication.
- **Implementation assistance:** software architecture, code drafting, testing support, editorial organization, and Recovery hardening performed through human–AI collaboration.

Machine assistance does not independently establish authorship, historical priority, or legal ownership. Repository history, receipts, external timestamps, and other evidence should be evaluated according to what each mechanism can actually prove.

When referencing the framework or software, use the metadata in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) and retain the following attribution:
