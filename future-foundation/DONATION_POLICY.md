# Draft Donation Policy

**Status:** Future policy. The project is **not currently accepting donations**.

~~~text
DONATIONS_ACCEPTED = FALSE
TAX_DEDUCTIBILITY_CLAIMED = FALSE
PAYMENT_REQUIRED_FOR_ACCESS = FALSE
~~~

If a future qualified charitable organization begins fundraising, the policy should require:

- gifts to organizational accounts, never personal accounts;
- no promise of investment return, equity, token appreciation, or governance;
- no purchase of canonical glyph status or authorship recognition;
- documentation of donor restrictions before acceptance;
- written acknowledgment processes appropriate to the applicable rules;
- separate handling for noncash and digital-asset gifts;
- rejection of gifts that threaten mission independence or create unacceptable obligations.

Future principle:

~~~text
DONATION != PURCHASE
DONOR != OWNER
DONATION != CANONICAL_CONTROL
ACCESS != DONATION
~~~
