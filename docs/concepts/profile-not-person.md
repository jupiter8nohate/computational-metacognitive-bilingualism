# Profile != Person

**Canonical definition.** PROFILE != PERSON means a stored, inferred, or generated data profile is a partial computational representation and must not be treated as the complete human being it describes.

## Formal expression

```text
PROFILE != PERSON
```

## Why it matters

Profiles simplify people into fields, scores, embeddings, labels, histories, or predictions. CMB keeps representation separate from human identity and self-definition.

## Example

A risk score may summarize selected variables for one task, but it should not become unquestioned authority over a person's character, worth, or complete identity.

## Interpretation boundaries

CMB does not prohibit profiles. It argues for scope, purpose limitation, appeal, uncertainty, and recognition that the person remains more than the representation.
