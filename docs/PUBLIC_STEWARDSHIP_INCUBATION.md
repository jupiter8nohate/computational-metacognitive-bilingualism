## Incubation rule

During this phase the project does not represent itself as a charity, foundation, trust, or tax-exempt organization.

It does not solicit tax-deductible donations and does not issue an investment token.

~~~text

TAX_EXEMPT_STATUS = NOT_CLAIMED
DONATIONS_ACCEPTED = FALSE
PAID_ACCESS = FALSE
TOKEN = NONE
TREASURY = NONE
~~~

The creator remains the declared author and steward of the existing corpus while future charitable governance is studied.

## Why this phase exists

The purpose is to discover the project's real governance needs through practice before creating a legal entity.

The project can develop:

- mission language;
- public-benefit criteria;
- decision records;
- board and succession concepts;
- intellectual-property maps;
- treasury safeguards;
- donation rules;
- digital-asset safeguards;
- conflict-of-interest rules;
- dissolution principles.

These documents are prototypes for later professional review. They are not adopted corporate bylaws, trust instruments, tax opinions, or legal advice.

## Repositioning trigger

The project should consider legal formation only when its creator and future stewards understand:

1. the mission well enough to lock it;
2. what assets and IP would or would not enter stewardship;
3. who should govern independently;
4. what activities require money;
5. what accounting and compliance burden is acceptable;
6. how conflicts, succession, removal, and dissolution should work;
7. whether public fundraising is actually needed.
