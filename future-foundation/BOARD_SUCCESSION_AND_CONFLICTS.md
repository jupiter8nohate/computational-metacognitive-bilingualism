# Draft Board, Succession, and Conflict Framework

**Status:** Future governance design only.

## Board concept

A mature public-benefit institution should not depend permanently on one person's unilateral control.

Research target:

- small, functional board;
- meaningful independent representation;
- relevant skills across governance, finance, technology, education, accessibility, research, and creative stewardship;
- defined terms and removal procedures;
- documented recusals for conflicts.

## Founder continuity

The founder may retain a permanent historical designation such as:

~~~text
FOUNDER
ORIGINAL CREATOR
FOUNDING STEWARD
~~~

That historical status should remain distinct from unlimited legal authority.

## Succession

The future organization should be able to continue if the founder is unavailable.

Succession planning should cover:

- provenance authority;
- registry maintenance;
- release signing;
- domain and repository recovery;
- records access;
- treasury access;
- board vacancies;
- emergency continuity.

## Conflict rule

~~~text
DISCLOSE
RECUSAL_WHEN_REQUIRED
DOCUMENT
INDEPENDENT_REVIEW
RECOVERY
~~~
