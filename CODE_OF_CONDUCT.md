# Code of Conduct

CMB welcomes technical criticism, policy disagreement, artistic disagreement, and evidence-based challenges.

Participants are expected to:

- address ideas, code, evidence, and behavior rather than degrading people;
- avoid harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, or targeted humiliation;
- respect privacy and consent;
- disclose material conflicts when presenting independent review or endorsement;
- distinguish fact, inference, hypothesis, opinion, metaphor, and unknown;
- report security problems responsibly rather than weaponizing them against contributors.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove participation that materially undermines those conditions.

Good-faith criticism is not a conduct violation. The project explicitly prefers reproducible criticism over untestable praise.

```text
DISAGREEMENT != HARASSMENT
CRITICISM != DISLOYALTY
PERSON != PROFILE
```
