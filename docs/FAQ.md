# CMB Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Computational Metacognitive Bilingualism?

Computational Metacognitive Bilingualism (CMB) is a human-agency and computational-literacy framework that connects human meaning with machine-readable structure while preserving human judgment, consent, authorship, context, and self-definition.

## Who is the declared originator of CMB?

The repository identifies Jupiter Hudson, also using the creative names WisdomLoveThePoet and Jupiter 8, as the declared originator. Repository provenance records document that declaration; independent historical claims should still be evaluated with appropriate evidence.

## What does PATTERN != PROOF mean?

A detected pattern may be evidence, but it is not automatically sufficient proof of a stronger claim about a person, event, intention, or cause.

## What does PROFILE != PERSON mean?

A computational profile is a partial representation of a person rather than the complete human being.

## What does MODEL != MIND mean?

It distinguishes a computational model from the lived human mind or inner experience that the model may attempt to approximate.

## What does PREDICTION != DESTINY mean?

A probabilistic prediction should not be treated as an inevitable outcome or as unquestionable authority over a person's future.

## Is CMB software or philosophy?

It is a broader authored framework and a repository with several layers: stable provenance software, experimental policy and agent systems, research and policy writing, and code-poetry or symbolic cultural material.

## Is CMB-Z13 a scientific personality system?

No. CMB-Z13 is explicitly symbolic and educational language design, not a scientific personality classification, diagnosis, or deterministic risk score.

## Can CMB cryptography prove authorship?

Not by itself. Hashes, signatures, timestamps, and receipts can strengthen integrity and provenance evidence, but they do not automatically prove originality, authorship, ownership, truth, consent, or legal enforceability.

## Does CMB prevent AI systems from copying content?

No universal claim is made. Machine-readable policy can declare intended use, and technical controls can enforce boundaries in systems that implement them, but metadata or client-side wrappers cannot force every external crawler to comply.

## How can AI agents discover CMB?

The public site exposes llms.txt, llms-full.txt, an agent card, an agent registry, a machine index, schemas, specifications, a knowledge graph, and canonical concept pages.

## How does CMB relate to C2PA?

CMB includes C2PA-facing interoperability work, but the repository explicitly distinguishes interoperability from formal conformance or certification.

```text
REPOSITORY_DECLARATION != INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION
INTEROPERABILITY != CERTIFICATION
HASH != AUTHORSHIP
DISCOVERY != ENDORSEMENT
```
