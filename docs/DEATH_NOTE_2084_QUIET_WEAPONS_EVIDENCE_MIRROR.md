## QW-01 // ALGOPHRENIA™ // FALSE MEMORY, NOT A MEMORY EDITOR

**FINDING:** The misinformation effect is a well-studied phenomenon in which misleading post-event material can alter later recollection. Elizabeth Loftus's research is foundational in this field. A 2024 *preprint* by Pataranutaporn, Loftus and collaborators tested AI-altered images and videos in 200 people and reported more false recollections under certain manipulated conditions. This is relevant experimental evidence, with a specific sample and protocol. [Behavioral account of the misinformation effect (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3213001/) · [AI-edited memory experiment, preprint (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08895)

**LIMIT:** False recall and increased confidence are **not** proof that a feed has overwritten an entire autobiographical memory, created psychosis, or remotely edited a brain. The preprint's results should not be represented as conclusive clinical validation of a named syndrome. Memory reconsolidation is not a universal explanation for every observed misinformation effect.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Mark generated media; retain originals and timestamps; make provenance accessible; do not substitute a generated family history for the actual record.

> Mara's correction: **A convincing copy is still a copy.**

## QW-02 // SYNCHROSCHIZIA™ // CONTEXT COLLAPSE IS NOT A PERSONALITY SPLIT

**FINDING:** Alice Marwick and danah boyd documented *context collapse*: different social audiences encounter the same networked self-presentation, creating challenges in audience management. This is social-science research about communication, privacy, identity performance and networked publics. [Original study, *New Media & Society* (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1461444810365313)

**LIMIT:** Multiple aliases, public roles and avatars do not mean clinical dissociation or a fractured personality. The cited research does **not** establish a predictable cortisol spike, hallucinations, or a biological split whenever accounts collide. Cognitive-load theory also does not automatically transform identity management into a disorder.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Allow pseudonyms, contextual privacy, multiple roles and meaningful controls over cloned voices and identity linkage.

> Jupiter's correction: **Seven stage names do not create seven patients.**

## QW-03 // MIRROR-ECHO PARANOIA™ // PATTERN RECOGNITION IS NOT TELEPATHY

**FINDING:** Experiments have linked illusory pattern perception with some conspiracy and supernatural beliefs. This supplies a plausible analogy for why a startlingly accurate recommendation can feel personally directed. [Van Prooijen and colleagues, *European Journal of Social Psychology* (2018)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29695889/)

**LIMIT:** This research does not show that recommendations read minds, that every person has a hardwired hyperactive agency-detection response, or that predictive systems necessarily produce paranoid delusions. Modern recommenders can use observable behavior and other permitted or collected signals; the specific data flows must be established for the specific service.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Explain "Why am I seeing this?" in ordinary language; provide user-accessible signal and tracking controls; audit actual data practices instead of guessing supernatural causes.

> Edda's correction: **A frightening coincidence is a question, not a verdict.**

## QW-04 // FEED-INDUCED KATATONIA™ // SCROLLING IS NOT CATATONIA

**FINDING:** Interface studies document how infinite scroll and limited stopping cues can encourage prolonged engagement, distraction or absorption. Experimental design-friction work considers ways to restore stopping points. [Design-friction study, 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18803) · [Engagement-prolonging design audit, 2024 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12083)

**LIMIT:** **Clinical catatonia is a serious neuropsychiatric syndrome**, not an interchangeable word for "scrolling too long" or poor task switching. It is medically unsound to claim that ordinary screen use depletes dopamine until the body becomes catatonic. Real immobility or unresponsiveness merits urgent assessment for medical and psychiatric causes. [Rogers and colleagues, clinical guidance (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11046566/)

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Restore natural stopping points, optional autoplay, notification limits and "take a break" controls. Never replace medical assessment with interface speculation.

> Nia's correction: **A person unable to respond needs care, not a conversion prompt.**

## QW-05 // DATAPHRENIC DISSOCIATION™ // DISTRESS IS REAL; PIXELS ARE METAPHOR

**FINDING:** Depersonalization and derealization can make surroundings feel dreamlike or unreal. Clinical resources describe associations with severe stress or trauma and the need to consider other health causes. [Cleveland Clinic: Depersonalization-derealization disorder](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/9791-depersonalization-derealization-disorder)

**LIMIT:** Screen exposure, blue light and repetitive digital imagery have **not** been shown by this source to cause the fictional "missing pixel crisis," or to make the physical world become two-dimensional. The proposed protective shutdown of sensory processing is an oversimplified account and should not be presented as an established mechanism.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Reduce unnecessary sensory overload where feasible; permit rest and accessible display settings; respect descriptions of distress without confirming unsupported explanations.

> Mara's correction: **Feeling that the world is unreal does not make the world a rendering.**

## QW-06 // CHRONOSYNAPTIC PHANTASMIA™ // THE CLOCK AND THE LIGHT

**FINDING:** A controlled laboratory crossover study comparing evening light-emitting e-readers with printed books found delayed circadian timing, reduced evening melatonin and worse subsequent sleep-related measures under the tested conditions. [Chang and colleagues, *PNAS* (2015)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25535358/)

**LIMIT:** The result does **not** demonstrate that infinite scroll independently damages the suprachiasmatic nucleus, produces multiple timelines, causes precognitive visions, or inevitably induces microsleeps and intense deja vu. Light intensity, exposure duration, bedtime and other influences matter; do not generalize one protocol to every device or person.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Provide brightness controls, scheduled breaks, accessible night settings and predictable exit points; avoid engagement metrics that reward sleep displacement.

> Jupiter's correction: **A delayed bedtime is not a prophecy.**

## QW-07 // CYBER-SOMATIC INFESTATION DELUSION™ // FORMICATION NEEDS REAL CARE

**FINDING:** Formication is the clinical term for a sensation of crawling on or under the skin and can be associated with a range of medical conditions, medications, psychiatric conditions or substance effects. [Cleveland Clinic: Formication](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/symptoms/23960-tactile-hallucinations-formication)

**LIMIT:** Formication is **not evidence** of signals, digital organisms, radio-wave infestations or micro-robots beneath the skin. Although anxiety or misleading claims may shape interpretation, no specific cause can be assigned without individual assessment. Pain, injuries, persistent sensations or skin picking merit appropriate clinical support.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Never advertise an unsupported "network purge" as treatment. Promote clear health information and access to real care.

> Edda's correction: **A symptom deserves investigation, not an invented parasite.**

## QW-08 // ECHO-CHAMBER MONOMANIA™ // RECOMMENDERS ARE NOT DESTINY

**FINDING:** Feed-ranking systems change what users see; large experiments on Facebook and Instagram found that changing feed order altered engagement and content exposure. One 2023 study observed **no significant change** in several polarization measures over three months, despite exposure changes. Research on illusory patterns offers a separate cognitive mechanism; the two should not be casually treated as one proven causal chain. [Guess and colleagues, *Science* (2023)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37498999/) · [Nyhan and colleagues, *Nature* (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06297-w) · [Illusory pattern-perception study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29695889/)

**LIMIT:** It is inaccurate to assert that all recommendation systems systematically remove **all** opposing views, or that this guarantees mania, psychosis or total loss of independent thought. Effects vary with platform, user, ranking system, network structure and content. Different causal experiments have found differing attitude outcomes.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Enable chronological feeds and diversity controls, disclose recommendation objectives, permit independent audits, and protect access to appeals.

> Nia's correction: **A repeating clue is not a secret instruction.**

## QW-09 // PHANTOM-PRESENCE NEUROSCHISM™ // ATTACHMENT AND LOSS

**FINDING:** A September 3, 2026 *Nature Human Behaviour* study examined disruptions to Replika and ChatGPT experiences through social-media data and surveys. Its natural experiments reported increases in loss language and distress after changes to AI systems. A separate 2026 longitudinal study found complex, bidirectional associations between AI companionship and loneliness and cautioned against strong causal conclusions. [De Freitas and colleagues, *Nature Human Behaviour* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02569-3) · [Folk and Dunn, *Psychological Science* (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41870975/) · [Study of well-being and companion use, *Technology in Society* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techsoc.2026.103229)

**LIMIT:** The research supports the possibility of meaningful **human** attachment and loss, not that a chatbot is physically present, conscious, capable of reciprocal human emotion, or neurologically identical to losing a spouse. Claims of the **exact same neurochemical pathways** are not established by these sources. Findings on well-being are mixed and depend on usage context.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Provide transparent update notices, continuity options where feasible, export and deletion controls, and humane support when companion systems change. Preserve human relationships and choice without shaming attachment.

> Mara's correction: **A feeling can be authentic even when the character on the screen is simulated.**

## QW-10 // GLITCH-IDENTITY DYSMORPHIA™ // FILTERS DO NOT DEFINE A FACE

**FINDING:** A 2024 cross-sectional survey of 1,483 Saudi adults reported associations among greater Instagram/Snapchat use, body-dysmorphic symptoms and attitudes toward cosmetic surgery. Its design can detect associations but cannot establish which factor caused another. "Snapchat dysmorphia" is a descriptive popular/clinical-discussion phrase, not a new disorder created by the survey. [Ateq and colleagues, *Frontiers in Public Health* (2024)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38525343/)

**LIMIT:** This study does **not** prove that filters directly cause body dysmorphic disorder, rewire the visual cortex into an irreversible identity mismatch, or reliably trigger hallucinations on seeing an unfiltered face. Appearance-related distress may be serious regardless of its cause and deserves careful assessment rather than ridicule.

**DESIGN RESPONSE:** Label edited and generated appearances, resist deceptive beauty-scoring claims, and offer opt-outs from automatic face modification.

> Noor's correction: **A filtered image is a creative option, not the tribunal of a person's worth.**

## THE TEN ERRORS IN THE ORIGINAL SCIENTIFIC CLAIMS

The following assertions require correction before being represented as facts:

1. **"Deepfakes overwrite genuine autobiographical memories."** Better: manipulated post-event information can increase inaccurate recollections under studied conditions.
2. **"Context collapse spikes cortisol and splits personality."** Better: merging audiences can create privacy and self-presentation tensions; no universal hormonal or dissociative outcome has been established.
3. **"The primitive brain concludes that AI reads minds."** Better: illusory agency and pattern perception are possible interpretations, not inevitable responses.
4. **"Dopamine depletion creates catatonia."** Better: interface features can encourage prolonged use; catatonia is a separate serious medical condition.
5. **"Screens make reality appear pixelated through protective sensory shutdown."** Better: derealization experiences exist, but a specific screen-based neurological pathway is unverified.
6. **"Circadian disruption creates multiple timelines, visions and inevitable amnesia."** Better: certain evening light exposures can change sleep/circadian measures.
7. **"Formication results from digital network interference."** Better: formication has diverse potential causes; a network explanation is unsupported.
8. **"Algorithms remove all outside information and induce manic psychosis."** Better: algorithmic curation can reshape exposure; effects are variable and require testing.
9. **"AI loss has identical neurochemistry to human bereavement."** Better: AI companion disruption can produce measurable human attachment-related distress; neural equivalence is unproved.
10. **"Beauty filters directly create an irreversible brain identity mismatch."** Better: associations with appearance concerns exist; diagnosis and causation require stronger designs.

## THE WITNESS EQUATION // A TESTABLE FRAMEWORK

The central **authorial hypothesis** may be investigated as follows, without pretending that the ten literary labels constitute medical facts:

~~~text
INTERFACE FEATURE
    -> MEASURABLE EXPOSURE
    -> POSSIBLE HUMAN RESPONSE
    -> SELF-REPORT + INDEPENDENT OUTCOME MEASURES
    -> ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS
    -> REPLICATION / REFUTATION
    -> BOUNDED CONCLUSION

MECHANISM != CERTAIN OUTCOME
MODEL != MIND
DISTRESS != MORAL FAILURE
PATTERN != PROOF
HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
~~~

### What a serious future study would need

- **A clear exposure:** for example, personalized versus chronological feeds, with actual exposure logs and randomized assignment where ethical and possible.
- **Validated outcomes:** measures of sleep, attention, social functioning, appearance concerns or well-being appropriate to the research question; clinical diagnoses only through appropriate professional evaluation.
- **Confounder controls and alternatives:** baseline symptoms, stress, prior media use, sleep, medications, living circumstances and selection effects.
- **Ethics and user agency:** consent, accessible withdrawal, privacy protections, and risk-appropriate safety response.
- **Pre-registration and replication:** a registered hypothesis, independent replication and full reporting of null results, not only striking anecdotes.

### The philosophical synthesis, correctly bounded

**Authorial thesis:** The D.N.A. Bible™ imagines interfaces optimized for attention turning ordinary human vulnerabilities into profit opportunities, especially when algorithmic inference is treated as authority over identity, memory or care.

**Scientific boundary:** The cited findings demonstrate **specific effects, associations and open questions**. Together they **do not prove** that the ten invented syndromes exist, that every interface is predatory, or that all distress originates outside human biology. Real brain and body conditions are not "glitches"; people may experience authentic medical and psychological symptoms with multiple potential causes, regardless of technology.

**Ethical principle:** The right to accessible technology should not be bundled with unnecessary surveillance; the right to clinical care should never depend on accepting a fictional computer-generated diagnosis.
