## 01 // THE QUESTION BEFORE THE VERDICT

In the fictional lighthouse, Nia had one possession the machine could not convert into an automatic sentence: the right to ask where a claim came from.

The engine declared her childhood drawing defective. It gave the same ruling a confidence score. It signed a report. Then another report cited the first.

Nia drew a crooked bird in the margin and wrote:

> If every witness works for the verdict, who is watching the witnesses?

The research companion answers that question with **traceable sources and explicit limits**, not with claims of infallibility.

## 02 // THE THREE-LAYER RECORD

Each witness statement has three layers:

1. **SOURCE:** An identifiable publication, rule, study, or agency action with an inspectable HTTPS URL.
2. **SUPPORTED CLAIM:** The narrow real-world mechanism or institutional practice that the source actually addresses.
3. **LIMITATION:** What must not be inferred from that evidence, including unsupported universalization or the fictional 2084 outcome.

~~~text
𓁹 WITNESS -> TRACE -> VERIFY -> LIMIT -> REVIEW

SOURCE_FOUND             != CLAIM_PROVEN
PRODUCT_DOCUMENTATION    != PREVALENCE_STUDY
REGULATORY_ALLEGATION    != FINAL_JUDGMENT
RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE       != MASS_DEPLOYMENT
STATISTICAL_SCORE        != PERSONHOOD
FICTIONAL_SCENARIO       != DOCUMENTED_EVENT
~~~

## 03 // HOW TO READ THE TEN RECORDS

The data file [datasets/2084-witness-evidence.v1.json](https://jupiter8nohate.github.io/computational-metacognitive-bilingualism/datasets/2084-witness-evidence.v1.json) contains **ten records**, numbered 01 through 10, parallel to the ten rows in the published [Evidence Ledger](DEATH_NOTE_2084_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md).

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| \`id\` | Stable table identifier from 01 to 10 |
| \`mechanism\` | Topic, not the name of a confirmed conspiracy |
| \`evidence_status\` | \`documented\`, \`context_dependent\`, or \`research\` |
| \`supported_claim\` | Precise summary of what is evidenced |
| \`limitation\` | Explicit negative boundary against overreach |
| \`sources[].title\` | Human-readable source name |
| \`sources[].url\` | Full source URL, also printed in the canonical article |
| \`sources[].source_type\` | Agency report, guidance, case, legal text, standard, vendor statement, or research |

**Status is about the specified claim, not an entire field.** A documented regulatory action does not mean every allegation has been proved in court. A vendor manual establishes its described feature, not its prevalence. A paper establishes its stated research result, not universal deployment.

## 04 // TWO DIFFERENT KINDS OF VERIFICATION

**Automated structural audit**

Run from the repository root:

~~~sh
python scripts/verify_2084_evidence.py
pytest -q tests/test_2084_witness_evidence.py
python scripts/build_docs.py
~~~

The standard-library verifier checks:

- Exactly ten distinct, ordered records.
- A preserved fiction boundary and a dated research snapshot.
- Recognized epistemic and source-type labels.
- Nonempty supported claims and explicit limitations.
- HTTPS source URLs without credentials or fragments.
- A corresponding numbered row and matching source URL in the public article.

The docs builder separately checks required public pages and their internal links. CI tests check deliberate failures: false certainty, uncited additions, missing limits, missing rows, and invalid URLs.

**Human source review**

These automated tests **do not** request external pages or establish that the URLs currently load, that agency guidance is unchanged, or that a research conclusion is sound. A reviewer must open each source, inspect its date and jurisdiction, read the context, distinguish complaints from decisions, consider counterevidence, and record corrections through a reviewable PR.

### Required reviewer questions

| Question | Failure to prevent |
| --- | --- |
| Who published this and when? | Unattributed or obsolete claims |
| Is it a complaint, final order, law, study, or vendor description? | Mistaking allegations or marketing for confirmed results |
| Does this source support the particular sentence? | Citation laundering |
| Are there exceptions, counterexamples, and jurisdiction limits? | Universalizing narrow evidence |
| Could an individual inspect, challenge, or correct the decision? | Proxy authority |
| Have we represented the fictional story as fictional? | Turning allegory into an allegation |

A pass is **proof of consistency of the tracked text fields**, not proof of every claim. A broken external source should be flagged for human correction, not replaced with invented evidence.

## 05 // THE AUTHOR'S CORRECTION RIGHT

Changes should preserve an audit trail through Git history and a reviewable pull request. Do not silently replace inconvenient evidence, invent an external validation date, auto-promote a research status to \`documented\`, or insert unsupported criminal or geopolitical allegations.

When a source is revised or withdrawn:

1. Identify the affected numbered record.
2. Quote or paraphrase the precise disputed claim within lawful excerpt limits.
3. Record the source, date, changed understanding, and counterevidence.
4. Update the article and JSON together in a single PR.
5. Preserve the limitation and literary boundary, then rerun tests.

The author retains the right to decide the literary meaning of the crooked bird. External reviewers retain the right to challenge empirical claims.

## 06 // THE EXISTENTIAL CLOSING

The original machine's fatal weakness was not an inability to count.

It could count every blink of a lighthouse. It could produce an archive of ten thousand pages. It could label its reports **VERIFIED** until the ink ran out of the universe.

What it lacked was the discipline to say:

**I MAY BE WRONG.**

That is the protocol.

The record must allow a human to look at the original source, ask a better question, and change the conclusion.

~~~text
𓂀 WITNESS://BACKTRACE
OBSERVATION != UNDERSTANDING
SIGNAL != SOURCE
PATTERN != PROOF
PROFILE != PERSON
MODEL != MIND
HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
~~~
