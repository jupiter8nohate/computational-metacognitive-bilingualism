# Draft Treasury and Digital-Asset Policy

**Status:** Future policy. No project treasury currently exists.

~~~text
TREASURY = NONE
PRODUCTION_WALLET = NONE
ACTIVE_FUNDRAISING = FALSE
~~~

If a future nonprofit creates a treasury, recommended control principles include:

- accounts titled in the legal organization's name;
- separation from founder and director personal finances;
- documented budgets and approvals;
- multiple-person authorization for material transfers;
- no seed phrases or private keys in GitHub;
- no single-person control of significant digital assets;
- independent reconciliation and reporting;
- explicit network and asset allow-lists;
- written incident and key-recovery procedures;
- no proprietary investment token merely to fund operations.

Illustrative future architecture:

~~~text
ORGANIZATION
    ↓
BOARD-APPROVED TREASURY
    ↓
MULTI-PERSON CONTROL
    ↓
DOCUMENTED PUBLIC-BENEFIT EXPENDITURE
~~~

A digital-asset wallet would be an organizational treasury tool, not proof of identity, ownership, or charitable status.
