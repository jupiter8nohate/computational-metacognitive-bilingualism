# 7. Ethics and Agency

Bio-silicon research combines living material, instrumentation, computation, and interpretation. CMB therefore treats governance and claim discipline as part of the technical architecture rather than as an afterthought.

## Authority boundary

```text
MACHINE_CAN = {
  observe,
  record,
  transform,
  classify,
  predict,
  compare,
}

MACHINE_DOES_NOT_ACQUIRE = {
  personhood_authority,
  moral_authority,
  identity_authority,
  consent_authority,
  semantic_ownership,
}
```

The presence of a biological signal does not by itself answer questions about moral status, welfare, consciousness, or personhood. Those questions require evidence, ethical analysis, and governance beyond a residual calculation.

## CMB rule

```text
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
OBSERVATION != UNDERSTANDING
MODEL != MIND
HUMAN_AGENCY > MACHINE_AUTHORITY
```

## Research governance

Any real biological research should follow the applicable institutional, legal, biosafety, ethics, consent, and oversight requirements of the relevant jurisdiction and research organization. This repository provides a software verification framework, not authorization to conduct biological experiments.
